Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How do I get a rental quote?

Send your travel date, pickup location, destination, passenger count and trip duration through the enquiry form. Pricing depends on your requirements and availability.

Are tolls, parking and interstate charges included?

These charges may be extra. Confirm the final inclusions and any applicable driver, night-halt or additional-distance charges when you receive your quote.

Can I enquire for a corporate outing or wedding?

Yes. Select your trip type in the form and include any useful details about the group and schedule.

Does submitting the form confirm my booking?

No. The form prepares a WhatsApp enquiry. Your booking is confirmed only after you discuss and agree on the trip details with Bharath Translines.
